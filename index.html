I am an undergraduate student in Information Engineering at the TUM School of Computation, Information and Technology (CIT), Technical University of Munich (TUM).

My research interests lie in Natural Language Processing, Large Language Models, and Computational Linguistics. I am particularly interested in understanding how language models reason with language and knowledge, how their linguistic and reasoning capabilities vary across languages and contexts, and where and why they systematically fail.

I am currently seeking PhD opportunities starting in Fall 2027 and would be excited to connect with researchers working on related topics.
